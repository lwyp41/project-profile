# Workflow — Deep

- [primary_flow] A request is configured, investigated, reviewed, then rendered.
- [actors] The user configures and approves; the Skill investigates and renders.
- [happy_path] Evidence recovery proceeds from discovery to a reviewed profile.
- [gates_handoffs] Review approval is the handoff before final rendering.
- [guarded_path] Material user-answerable gaps pause for clarification.
- [public_entries] Mode selection and Custom onboarding are distinct public entries.
- [state_handoffs] Facts pass from extraction to reconstruction, interview, review, and rendering.
- [approval_gate] Review approval is required unless an unattended run was explicitly requested.
- [source_boundary] Repository artifacts support facts but do not prove adoption or ownership.
- [fallback_degradation] Unknown remains visible when evidence cannot answer a material question.
- [separation_rationale] The Review gate separates evidence reconstruction from prose composition.
- [failure_prevention] Clarification-required status prevents unsupported rationale from becoming fact.
