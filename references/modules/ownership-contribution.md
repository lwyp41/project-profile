# Ownership and Contribution

## Goal

Recover personal scope, collaborators, AI assistance, maintenance responsibility, and the attribution boundary.

## Applicability and depth

Enable when a downstream consumer could mistake possession or commits for authorship. Collaboration uncertainty is clarification, not N/A.

- **Brief:** inspect attribution statements, authorship metadata, and overview.
- **Standard:** inspect commits, releases, issues, contribution guides, and documentation for named scope.
- **Deep:** compare attribution across sources and reconstruct design, implementation, research, validation, coordination, and maintenance separately.

## Facts, interview, and rendering

Record actor, contribution verb, artifact/decision, period, collaboration/AI boundary, source, and confidence. Ask which parts were personally decided, built, validated, or maintained. Commits, account names, and repository location do not prove full ownership; render neutral scoped attribution.

## Render by depth

- **Brief render:** supported actor and scoped contribution boundary.
- **Standard render:** preserve Brief; add named artifacts or decisions, collaboration/AI boundary, period, and confidence caveat.
- **Deep render:** preserve Standard; separate evidenced design, implementation, research, validation, coordination, and maintenance contributions; reconcile attribution conflicts and surface material gaps.
