# Project Overview

## Goal and applicability

Recover name, lifecycle, shape, main capability, and observable boundary. It is normally applicable; missing adoption or ownership is not a reason to invent either.

## Investigation by depth

- **Brief:** inspect entry points, metadata, README, and one representative artifact.
- **Standard:** inspect package/configuration, examples, and history for scope and lifecycle.
- **Deep:** reconcile historical names, releases, boundaries, and conflicting descriptions.

## Facts, interview, and rendering

Record lifecycle, capability, artifact, source, and caveat. Ask only when identity or lifecycle changes interpretation. Do not turn artifact existence into adoption or ownership; render a compact neutral orientation.
