# Users and Stakeholders

## Goal and applicability

Distinguish intended users, confirmed actual users, owners, collaborators, and affected parties. N/A is valid only when no human or system stakeholder exists.

## Investigation by depth

- **Brief:** inspect README and permissions/ownership statements.
- **Standard:** inspect feedback, issues, examples, access configuration, and documentation.
- **Deep:** trace stakeholder changes, evidence of actual use, conflicts, and affected-party risks.

## Facts, interview, and rendering

Record role, intended/actual status, source, time, and caveat. Ask who actually used or was affected when material. Missing adoption evidence is neither proof of zero users nor permission to claim them; render intended and actual users separately.
