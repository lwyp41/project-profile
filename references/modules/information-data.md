# Information and Data

## Goal and applicability

Recover sources of truth, provenance, schemas, isolation, retrieval/storage, generated-output policy, and privacy boundaries.

## Investigation by depth

- **Brief:** inspect data/source policy and primary schemas or templates.
- **Standard:** inspect configuration, scripts, examples, validation, and privacy rules.
- **Deep:** trace source-to-output flow, access boundaries, retention, generated-text reuse, and historical policy changes.

## Facts, interview, and rendering

Record source role, authority, transformation, storage/access boundary, policy, and caveat. Ask about material retention or source authority gaps. Do not let research context or generated text become factual evidence without an explicit rule; render the firewall clearly.

## Render by depth

- **Brief render:** primary source of truth, data role, and output boundary.
- **Standard render:** preserve Brief; add transformation, storage/access boundary, validation or privacy rule, and important caveat.
- **Deep render:** preserve Standard; add source-to-output flow, authority and access boundaries, retention, generated-text reuse policy, historical policy changes, and unresolved source gaps where evidenced.
